The parent & learner checklist

The right questions. A more informed decision.

01

Course fit & starting level

Who is the programme for? What prior skills are needed? Ask how an entry diagnostic can identify a suitable starting point.

02

What the learner studies

Review actual modules, recommended hours, practice activities, learning outcomes and the course assessment approach.

03

Fees & enrolment terms

Ask for confirmed batches, fees, materials, attendance conditions and any applicable cancellation or refund terms before enrolling.

04

Support & realistic outcomes

Discuss centre contact, age-appropriate learning and feedback. No automatic job, admission, exam score or visa guarantee.

Which English level should I choose?

Use a short speaking, listening, reading and writing diagnostic. You may enter at an appropriate level rather than starting with Foundation.

Who issues language examination certificates?

JLPT, Goethe and HSK examinations are external credentials. NimbusWorld training is separate; confirm registration and certificate requirements.

What are the fees and class timings?

Ask the authorised team for approved fees, batches, teaching format and enrolment terms. No unconfirmed schedule or price is published here.

Are the industry programmes currently available?

Hospitality, travel and luxury retail pathways are proposed and subject to partner approval. Ask the team about their current status before enrolling.

Are jobs, admissions or visas guaranteed?

No. Courses build relevant skills; decisions by employers, institutions, exam bodies and government authorities remain independent.
